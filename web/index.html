FAÍSCA / NOISEBOX FOR THE FM-1

Four loops
that sing.

A free, open firmware that turns the FM-1 into a noisebox: four feedback loops joined by a send matrix, a fixed chain of twelve effects inside every loop, played with the hands. There is no oscillator: every sound is the network held at the edge where it starts to ring.

03 — The sound

One chain.
Always the same.

Each of the four loops runs the same fixed chain, in this order, and writes the result into its delay line, which feeds the loop again one period later. The fourth knob of every effect is its MIX; at zero the effect is not in the path.

The loops are tuned to partials of MID, from 20 Hz to 10 kHz. GAIN sets how much each loop gives back: below unity the loops ring when struck and fade; above it they sing on their own, and the preamp's saturation holds them there. CROSS scales the 4 × 4 matrix of signed sends between the loops; SCREAM lets the output bend the loops' own delay times. A limiter that cannot be switched off keeps the output at or below −3 dBFS. The theory explains the physics.

04 — Orientation

Five rules.
One map.

Learn these once and you know the whole instrument. The manual has the map of every control.

  1. 01Three knobs never changeCROSS, SCREAM and GAIN, and LEVEL on the pot.
  2. 02One button, one sectionK1 to K4 play the lit section: LOOP, CHAN, MATRIX, SHAPE, FX, DELAY, REVERB, MOD. CHAN, MATRIX and FX step on when pressed again.
  3. 03Holding is a layerMEMORY and STORE: the sixteen sounds on the white keys. FX: the twelve effects. LFO: the sources, and a turned knob gets a depth. RIBBON: the keys become positions.
  4. 04The fourth knob is MIXIn every effect. At zero the effect is not in the path.
  5. 05The screen says what you touchedName and value, large, for a moment; then the four lamps.

05 — MIDI

The same map
everywhere.

The map lives in the shared core, so the emulator and the firmware read MIDI the same way. Channel: any, or one of 1 to 16. Note-on with velocity 0 counts as note-off.

In the emulator, the MIDI panel under the instrument chooses the input and output ports and the channel, and keeps the bindings made by MIDI learn (Alt-click a control, then move a MIDI control): clear, export and import them as JSON. The output sends the knobs as CC and the lamps as notes on the same map, for controllers that can show them.

03 — O som

Uma cadeia.
Sempre a mesma.

Cada um dos quatro laços passa pela mesma cadeia fixa, nesta ordem, e grava o resultado na sua linha de atraso, que alimenta o laço de novo um período depois. O quarto knob de todo efeito é o MIX; no zero, o efeito não está no caminho.

Os laços se afinam em parciais do MID, de 20 Hz a 10 kHz. GAIN define quanto cada laço devolve: abaixo da unidade os laços ressoam quando golpeados e somem; acima dela cantam sozinhos, e a saturação do pré os segura ali. CROSS escala a matriz 4 × 4 de envios com sinal entre os laços; SCREAM deixa a saída curvar os tempos de atraso dos próprios laços. Um limitador que não se desliga mantém a saída em −3 dBFS ou abaixo. A teoria explica a física.

Before installing

Can I go back to the official firmware?

Yes, with the official workbench. The recovery page has every way back, from the mildest to the strongest.

Which browser do I need?

The emulator plays in any recent browser; its MIDI needs Chrome or Edge. Installing on the device needs Chrome or Edge on a computer (Web MIDI and WebUSB), not a phone.

Will I lose my sounds and projects?

Back them up with the official workbench before installing anything. Each release will say exactly which part of the unit's memory FAÍSCA writes and what it leaves untouched.

The installer does not find my unit.

Use a USB cable that carries data, connect the unit directly (not through a hub), switch it on, close other pages or programs that use MIDI, and allow MIDI access when the browser asks.

Do I need an FM-1 to try it?

No. The emulator is the same core and plays in the browser.

Antes de instalar

Posso voltar ao firmware oficial?

Sim, pelo workbench oficial. A página de recuperação tem todos os caminhos de volta, do mais leve ao mais forte.

Qual navegador eu preciso?

O emulador toca em qualquer navegador recente; o MIDI dele pede Chrome ou Edge. Instalar no aparelho pede Chrome ou Edge num computador (Web MIDI e WebUSB), não no celular.

Vou perder meus sons e projetos?

Faça o backup pelo workbench oficial antes de instalar qualquer coisa. Cada versão dirá exatamente que parte da memória da unidade o FAÍSCA grava e o que deixa intacto.

O instalador não encontra a minha unidade.

Use um cabo USB que passe dados, ligue a unidade direto (sem hub), deixe-a ligada, feche outras páginas ou programas que usem MIDI e permita o acesso MIDI quando o navegador pedir.

Preciso de um FM-1 para experimentar?

Não. O emulador é o mesmo núcleo e toca no navegador.
